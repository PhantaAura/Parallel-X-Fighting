# Parallels X: Clash of Souls — Prototype 2.9A.15

## Touch combat comfort

- Rebuilt the default portrait and landscape control maps around minimum 44-pixel action targets.
- Kept the joystick and every button inside the visible safe area on small phones.
- Separated Light, Heavy, Grab, Charge, Dash, Launcher, Breaker, Throw, Pause, Settings, and utility controls.
- Moved the portrait battle view above the action zone and kept controls clear of the ability hotbar.
- Hid desktop control-guide and footer text during touch matches.
- Migrated older default touch layouts to the new schema while preserving deliberate custom layouts.

## Phone menus

- Added a compact portrait main menu with separate header, mode grid, preview, and footer regions.
- Added a short-landscape menu that exposes every available choice without a hidden inner scroll area.
- Changed first-load touch prompts from keyboard language to tap and swipe language.
- Added an explicit swipe cue to the Classic quick-match settings rail.
- Raised character-select buttons and selectors above the standard 44-pixel comfort target.

## Controller compatibility

- Preserved Nintendo, Xbox, PlayStation, custom mappings, two-player assignments, controller prompts, pause, reconnect, and D-pad menu navigation.
- The comfort changes affect presentation only and do not change combat timing, damage, mappings, or Story progression.
