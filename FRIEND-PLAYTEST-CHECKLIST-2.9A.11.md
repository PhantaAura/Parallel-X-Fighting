# Prototype 2.9A.11 Friend Playtest Checklist

## Before testing

- Force-refresh the site.
- Confirm the main menu says **Prototype 2.9A.11 — Bark, Wade & Tournament Pacing**.
- Start or continue Rrvvfo’s Story and enter Chapter 2.

## Sprite checks

- Confirm Bark has shaggy black hair, black/tan clothing, and his intended skin tone.
- Confirm Wade has very spiky blond hair and blue/yellow clothing.
- Watch Bark and Wade idle and walk in the hub.
- Test Bark and Wade in Arena and Story fights.
- Check feet for sliding, sudden size changes, cropped effects, rough scaling, or frames containing sheet debris.
- Test both sharp and smoothed sprite-quality settings.

## Tournament sequence

- Watch the opening ceremony and confirm every important contestant is introduced.
- Confirm Hailey versus Plouke occurs before Rrvvfo’s first official fight.
- Fight Hamual and confirm his size difference is obvious without hiding the HUD or hit effects.
- Fight Daniel and confirm he feels technical rather than like another generic opponent.
- Watch the full Bark versus Pouki sequence and verify all three phases trigger.
- Confirm Rrvvfo versus Wade and the Plouke final still occur in the correct order.

## Hub and intermissions

- Recover the fan card through dialogue.
- Find Bark’s card on the upper market walkway.
- Catch the unreadable card on the moving maintenance cart.
- Verify the three cracked-ring supports give different evidence.
- Confirm nearby speech bubbles appear without replacing combat notices.
- After each official event, inspect the medical tent, crowd positions, arena repair descriptions, bracket, and food stand.

## Plouke preparation

- Complete all four playable Plouke-study activities.
- Try a wrong answer and confirm the activity explains the trap and lets you retry.
- Verify the bracket board blocks the next event until the required clue is complete.
- Before the final, confirm the preparation bench shows level, HP, clue count, meal status, and save confirmation.

## Gameplay repairs

- Start Wade’s race and confirm the player cannot move during 3–2–1.
- Check target time, checkpoint splits, lead text, and best-time display.
- Finish once, talk to Wade again, and retry the race.
- In Dummy on the Loose, try defeating the dummy without all three mechanics; it should remain active.
- Complete one Perfect Parry, one launch-to-Pursuit, and one Grab while the dummy blocks, then finish it.
- Gain optional XP and verify major opponents do not simply stay one or two levels above Rrvvfo.

## Plouke performance

- Reach the fatigue scene by strong damage and compare it with a slower attempt.
- Push Plouke near his protected HP threshold and verify crowd feedback changes.
- Lose the beam clash after a strong performance and check for the special dialogue, XP, and performance badge.
- Win the beam clash and confirm its larger reward and ring-out ending still work.

## Most useful feedback

For every problem, include the area or fight, input device, objective text, expected result, actual result, and a screenshot or short recording when possible.
