# Prototype 2.9A.34 — Signature Combat & Impact

## Fighter identity
- Rrvvfo earns an **Improvised Angle** moment by landing a close hit during the short window after Object Swap.
- Revvfo earns **Relentless Pressure** after three close connected actions without letting pressure expire.
- Wade earns a **Lightning Near-Miss** by dashing through an active strike without being hit.
- Bark earns an **Armored Punish** by absorbing a hit with startup/dash armor and answering before the punish window closes.
- These moments emphasize decisions and resource flow rather than adding raw damage multipliers.

## Combat impact
- Match-ending KOs now receive fighter-colored final-hit treatment, stronger but accessibility-aware hit freeze, camera emphasis, impact audio, and a longer victory hold.
- Ordinary round KOs retain the existing presentation so the final knockout remains special.

## Training
- Added one short identity trial for each finished standard fighter.
- Trials use the real match mechanics and reset through the existing Arena Training flow.

## Compatibility
- Story scripting, stage rules, Quick Battle, damage scaling, and save data remain compatible.
- Save schema remains **268**.
