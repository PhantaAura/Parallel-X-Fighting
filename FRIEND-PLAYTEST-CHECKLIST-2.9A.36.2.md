# Friend Playtest Checklist — Prototype 2.9A.36.2

## False-completion recovery
1. Use a save where Chapters 1–3 are complete and Chapter 4 is incorrectly marked complete.
2. Open Rrvvfo's route.
3. Confirm Chapter 4 shows **SAVE REPAIR NEEDED** or offers **START FRESH**.
4. Choose **START FRESH** and confirm the warning.
5. Confirm Chapter 4 begins at Lower Echo Region.
6. Open the Story Menu and confirm it does not say `REPLAY (DOES NOT SAVE)`.
7. Reach Echo Village, exit the chapter, and continue again.
8. Confirm the real Chapter 4 checkpoint was saved.

## Preservation
- Confirm Chapters 1–3 are still complete.
- Confirm Story level, XP, and bonus stats are unchanged.
- Confirm settings and control layouts are unchanged.

## Real Replay
1. On a genuinely completed Chapter 4 save, select **REPLAY**.
2. Confirm the Story Menu says **REPLAY (DOES NOT SAVE)**.
3. Confirm **START CHAPTER 4 FRESH** appears as a separate choice.
4. Exit Replay and confirm the completed save remains intact.
