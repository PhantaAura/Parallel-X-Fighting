**A 2.5D arena fighter and RPG built around kinetic movement, pursuit combos, parries, character techniques, exploration, side quests, and continuous Story progression.**

Play the GitHub Pages build: <https://phantaaura.github.io/Parallel-X-Fighting/>

## Story progression

Select **STORY** and choose Rrvvfo to begin or continue. Normal chapter completions load the next playable Story section automatically.

- Chapter 1: Shots of Agony training, the Combat Manual, combat refresher, and a wooded Tournament Road
- Chapter 2: martial-arts festival hub, preparation quests, optional activities, intermissions, bracket fights, and Plouke
- Chapter 3: after-hours investigation, Resonance Facility, Unfinished Echo, Project Hollow, and the teleporter escape
- Chapter 4: Echo Village, team restoration missions, optional Ryuzankaro secret boss, mountain ascent, Hollow Watcher, and Shadow’s Lookout

Chapter 4 completion represents **67%** of Rrvvfo’s planned six-chapter route.

## Main controls

- Move/depth: **W / A / S / D**
- Jump: **Space**
- Light: **Left Click** or **J**
- Heavy: **K**
- Launcher: **I**
- Grab: **U**
- Dash: **Shift**
- Block: **Right Click** or **L**
- Charge: **C**
- Combo Breaker: **R**
- Counter: **Q**
- Interact: **E**
- Activate selected ability: **O**
- Ability slots: **1–5**
- Lens shortcut: **Z**
- Vibration Sense after unlocking: **V** or ability slot 4 while exploring Chapter 4
- Sage’s Manual: **M**
- Objective tracker: **T**
- Pause/Story menu: **Escape**
- Hub camera: mouse/trackpad movement or controller right stick
