# Revisit Loop & Exploration Rewards — 2.9A.40.6

## Purpose
Completed areas should not become dead content. This patch adds a reusable revisit layer on top of the connected-world architecture.

## Revisit opportunities
- Chapter 1: Cliff Precision Cache — gated by Precision Lock.
- Chapter 2: Rooftop Challenger — post-clear hidden rematch.
- Chapter 3: Sealed Service Archive — reserved by the framework for a later active revisit interaction.
- Chapter 4: Old Shrine Resonance — gated by Vibration Sense.

## Fast travel
After the relevant chapter is complete, the Story route menu can return to Tournament Plaza or Echo Village without entering Chapter Replay. These revisits preserve the real Story save.

## Persistence
Revisit rewards and travel nodes live inside connected-world state version 3. Existing save schema remains 268.
