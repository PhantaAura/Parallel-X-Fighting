# Friend Playtest Checklist — 2.9A.28.1

Test on an iPhone Home Screen installation and normal Safari where possible.

## Every Story hub

- The top of the screen shows one compact objective and one menu button.
- Tapping the objective information button expands and collapses the details.
- The objective and menu button do not cover the playable character or touch controls.
- The small persistent minimap is absent.
- The Map button still opens the complete map.

## Story menu

- The menu opens from the compact menu button.
- The sheet uses the full safe width and can scroll smoothly.
- Return to Game is easy to reach without scrolling through the entire journal.
- Technique cards can scroll sideways.
- Text is not cut off by the notch, Dynamic Island, browser bar, or Home indicator.

## Chapter-specific checks

- Chapter 1: Story Menu, Area Map, Sage Manual, and roadside defeat options work.
- Chapter 2: quest journal is readable in one column and the active-fight restart button still works.
- Chapter 3: Case Board uses the available screen and closes correctly.
- Chapter 4: Mission Journal and Story Menu use the spacious layout and restore gameplay after closing.

## Regression checks

- Desktop menus still use the normal desktop presentation.
- Combat health, energy, guard, and hotbar remain unchanged.
- Mode Select and Route Select still use the 2.9A.28 carousel layout.
