# Prototype 2.9A.40.5 — World Delight, Character Charm & Combat Feel

## Goal

Make the existing connected-world foundation feel warmer, more reactive, and more satisfying without adding another heavyweight progression system or touching Chapter 5.

## World Delight

- Added persistent World Delight discoveries across all four released chapters.
- Chapter 1 cliff-route completion now has a scenic high-road reaction.
- Chapter 2 Wade shortcuts now get a festival-route discovery reaction.
- Chapter 3's after-hours service shortcut gets a different nighttime/mystery reaction.
- Chapter 4 Old Water Lift and Old Apothecary Passage now get party/world reactions.
- Added a subtle Echo Village overlook discovery. Its marker only appears when the player is already close so it still feels found rather than assigned.
- World Delight discoveries are one-time discoveries, not grindable checklist rewards.

## Character charm

- Added short checkpoint banter around travel, tournament progress, Project Hollow discovery, Bark/Wade reunion, village defense, and the solo mountain transition.
- Banter is deliberately brief so it does not stop exploration every few steps.
- Field-skill mastery now includes a short owner reaction instead of only a technical journal card.

## Combat feel

- Flow Cancel gets stronger hit-freeze, camera impulse, impact flash, and a dedicated Story callout.
- Perfect parry feedback gets a slightly stronger freeze/shake response while still honoring accessibility scales.
- Pursuit finishers now emit their own Story combat-feedback event.
- Chapter 4 Support interruption gets a stronger burst, brief hitstop/camera response, and a dedicated callout.
- Hollow Watcher phase changes are more theatrical and Rrvvfo reacts to the escalation without changing the boss rules.
- Chapter 4 squad combat warns when Bark/Wade are in danger or knocked down.
- A clean village-defense clear with all three ninjas standing gets a short team reaction.

## Save / compatibility

- Save schema remains 268.
- Added `pxFieldSkillsV1` and `pxWorldDelightV1` to save export/import preservation.
- Chapters 1–4 progression and connected-world state are preserved.
- Chapter 5 remains untouched.
