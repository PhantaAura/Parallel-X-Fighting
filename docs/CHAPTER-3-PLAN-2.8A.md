# Chapter 3 — CLOSED OFF (working title)

## Full chapter target

1. Return to the expanded Training Region.
2. Explore several closed districts in a non-linear order.
3. Inspect destroyed roads and question residents.
4. Meet the strange man after gathering enough evidence.
5. Enter the underground base.
6. Solve three connected power-routing puzzle wings.
7. Reactivate the teleporter.
8. Enter the sealed destination.
9. Discover that the closures are part of a plan to trap everyone while a planetary ray charges.
10. Disable the ray network, defeat minibosses, reach the main weapon, defeat the chapter boss, and reopen the region.

## Build split

- 2.8A: Expanded region and investigation opening
- 2.8B: Underground base and three puzzle wings
- 2.8C: Teleporter destination and ray-network reveal
- 2.8D: Final weapon area, boss, aftermath, and post-chapter hub changes

The final combined chapter should be measured by real playtests. No runtime claim is made for 2.8A.
