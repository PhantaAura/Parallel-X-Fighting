# Friend Playtest Checklist — Prototype 2.9A.35

Test with a fresh save first. The main question is not only whether progression works, but whether the player has enough time to understand and care about each place.

## Chapter 1
1. Confirm the Training Grounds and Tournament Road feel like a beginning, not a long tutorial.
2. Choose one road route and complete the transport sequence.
3. Confirm the chapter does not add forced filler or unnecessary backtracking.

## Chapter 2
1. Enter the festival and do not follow a marker immediately. Explore naturally.
2. Confirm registration remains unavailable until three districts—including Central Plaza—are visited and two people are met.
3. Confirm the objective clearly reports progress instead of hiding the requirement.
4. Check that side activities appear gradually rather than all at once.
5. After a tournament round, walk around during the aftermath and check NPC reactions.
6. Confirm the player is never forced to stand still during the aftermath.

## Chapter 3
1. Compare the closed grounds with the Chapter 2 festival.
2. Visit the Main Arena and one other district.
3. Confirm the medical lead appears only after that orientation.
4. Check that optional investigations appear as the mystery develops.
5. Discover Project Hollow and verify the game gives the moment room before pushing the next objective.

## Chapter 4
1. Reach Echo Village and verify Bark and Wade do not appear immediately.
2. Inspect the Resonance Wall and Water Channel in either order.
3. Confirm Bark and Wade arrive only after both interactions.
4. Complete the village defense and explore during the recovery beat.
5. Verify the next route still opens and Ryuzankaro remains optional.

## Save and replay
1. Export and re-import a schema-268 save.
2. Resume an older Chapter 4 save that already reached Bark and Wade.
3. Confirm it does not demand the new landmark introduction again.
4. Refresh during a hub phase and check that objective and quest-wave progress return correctly.

## Feedback questions
- Did any chapter feel rushed?
- Did any chapter feel slow for the wrong reason?
- Which quiet aftermath made an event feel more important?
- Which objective felt like a checklist rather than an adventure?
- Were optional activities easy to discover without covering the map in markers?
