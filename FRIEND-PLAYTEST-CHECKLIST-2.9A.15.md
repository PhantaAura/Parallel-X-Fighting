# Prototype 2.9A.15 Friend Playtest

## Phone portrait

- Open the main menu on a small phone and confirm the logo, six available choices, preview, and touch hint do not overlap.
- Enter Training and confirm the battle view stays above the touch controls.
- Confirm no control is clipped and no two controls overlap.
- Confirm Charge, Breaker, and Throw remain clear of the ability hotbar.
- Confirm desktop keyboard instructions do not appear beneath the touch match.

## Phone landscape

- Test a short display similar to 667×375 and a wider display similar to 844×390.
- Confirm every main-menu choice is visible without guessing that an inner panel scrolls.
- Confirm Breaker and Throw are separated.
- Confirm the joystick, combat controls, and ability hotbar remain usable.

## Character select

- Confirm MODE SELECT, MOVE LIST, selectors, and fight buttons are comfortable to tap.
- Swipe the quick-match settings rail and confirm the on-screen cue explains the gesture.
- Confirm no page-wide horizontal scrolling appears.

## Controllers and desktop

- Test Nintendo, Xbox, or PlayStation input if hardware is available.
- Confirm the correct button labels appear and D-pad menu navigation, pause, disconnect, and reconnect still work.
- Confirm desktop keyboard and mouse prompts remain unchanged.
