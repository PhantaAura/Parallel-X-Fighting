# Prototype 2.9A.31 — Core Feel & Character Fantasy

## Core feel
- Basic Light, Heavy, and Launcher inputs pressed during the final recovery frames are retained and begin as soon as the fighter is legal to act.
- Fighter-specific dash-cancel and recovery values exaggerate movement rhythm without changing damage.
- Normal impacts layer fighter-specific audio and feedback: ember, warp, lightning, earth, phantom, and shift identities.

## Character fantasy
- Hurt, landing, and victory moments now use each fighter’s visual identity.
- Wade is the snappiest mover; Bark has the heaviest impact and commitment; Revvfo feels aggressive and slippery; Rrvvfo retains flexible improvisation.
- Arena Training includes a Fighter Identity comparison guide.

## Replay flow
- Results now prioritize Instant Rematch.
- Random Rematch chooses a new legal fighter pairing and playable stage without returning through setup screens. It is hidden during Story encounters so route progression cannot be replaced by a random battle.

## Safety
- Damage, energy costs, save schema, Story progression, Focus Recovery, and Pursuit Tech balance are unchanged.
