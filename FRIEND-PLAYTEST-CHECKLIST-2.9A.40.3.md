# Friend Playtest Checklist — 2.9A.40.3

This build is about whether the world starts feeling understandable and explorable, not whether every future interior/secret already exists.

## Fresh save

- Start Chapter 1 and open MAP.
- Check LOCAL / REGION / WORLD tabs.
- Confirm future regions are not spoiled.
- Take a Tournament Road branch and see whether the map learns it.
- Try the Cliff route and confirm the shortcut/discovery makes sense.

## Tournament Region

- Wander instead of immediately following the Story objective.
- Check whether Central Plaza feels like it has several meaningful directions.
- Complete the Wade route and check whether new district cuts are remembered.
- Enter Chapter 3 and confirm the after-hours tournament still feels geographically recognizable.

## Chapter 3 continuity

- Open the map at the tournament, inside Resonance Facility, and after the teleporter.
- Confirm the Region view changes appropriately.
- Make sure the World view communicates the journey without revealing inaccessible locations.

## Chapter 4 nonlinear routing

- Explore Echo Village before doing the next objective.
- After Wade's field skill is available, try the Old Water Lift in both directions.
- Start the optional old-man/potion route and look for the Old Apothecary Passage after Precision Lock is available.
- Ask: did the shortcut feel like something you discovered, or like another mandatory glowing marker?
- Leave and return; confirm opened shortcuts remain mapped.

## Save/reload stress

At least once, reload after:

- entering a new district;
- opening a permanent shortcut;
- entering Resonance Facility;
- arriving in Echo Region.

The map should remember the discovered geography without falsely completing Story objectives.

## What to report

The most useful feedback is:

- “This hub still feels like one road.”
- “I couldn't tell these districts connected.”
- “This shortcut isn't actually useful.”
- “The map spoiled something I hadn't found.”
- “I got lost, but not in a fun way.”
- “I found a route without the game telling me and that felt good.”
