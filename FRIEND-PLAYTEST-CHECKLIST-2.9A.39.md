# Prototype 2.9A.39 — First-Play RPG Checklist

Play from a fresh save if possible. Do not tell the player where optional activities are unless they are truly stuck.

## Whole-route questions
- Does something meaningful change roughly every 2–4 minutes: combat, dialogue, discovery, activity, route decision, world reaction, or new mechanic?
- Mark any stretch where the player says “I’m just walking” or asks what they are supposed to care about.
- Do S/A fight-rank reactions feel rewarding without interrupting the Story?
- Does a low rank make the player want to improve rather than feel forced to replay?

## Chapter 1 — 35–50 min target
- Does training turn into an adventure quickly?
- Are the road route, transport rescue, cart sequence, and road fight varied enough?
- Is any tutorial information repeated after the player already proved they understand it?

## Chapter 2 — 70–100 min target
- Does the festival feel different before registration, during the bracket, and between rounds?
- After a tournament fight, does the player naturally want to look around before the next match?
- Are mandatory activities fun enough to deserve being mandatory?
- Does the final feel like the payoff to a full tournament day instead of another fight node?

## Chapter 3 — 55–80 min target
- Can the player explain what clue they are following at each stage?
- Does the empty tournament ground create mystery rather than boredom?
- Does evidence reconstruction feel like deduction instead of memorizing a menu answer?
- Is the facility section noticeably more urgent than the investigation hub?

## Chapter 4 — 90–120 min target
- Does Echo Village feel worth learning before the attack begins?
- Do Bark/Wade/Rrvvfo field actions feel like teamwork rather than three copies of one interaction?
- After village defense, try **REST WITH BARK & WADE**. Confirm HP, Energy, and Guard refill and the moment feels optional.
- Confirm the mountain gate remains usable even if the party-rest interaction is ignored.
- Does the solo mountain section feel like a deliberate mood shift after the party sections?
- Does Hollow Watcher teach its adaptation instead of feeling randomly resistant?
- Does the summit pebble → floating Lookout Object Swap feel like a Chapter-ending technique payoff?

## Bug checks
- Save and reload at least once in each chapter.
- Open/close Story Menu and Journal in Chapter 4 several times.
- Confirm Chapter 4 does not become complete before the real Shadow Lookout ending.
- Replay a completed chapter and confirm permanent Story progress is preserved.
