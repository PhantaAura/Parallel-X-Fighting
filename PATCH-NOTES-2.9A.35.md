# Prototype 2.9A.35 — Living Hubs & RPG Pacing

## Goal
Make the first four Story chapters take enough time to matter without stretching them through fetch quests, slow movement, repeated fights, or forced waiting.

## Shared RPG pacing layer
- Added a save-safe chapter pacing model with five readable phases: Arrival, Development, Crisis, Aftermath, and Departure.
- First-visit orientation is based on places visited, people met, or meaningful landmarks inspected—not a hidden timer.
- Optional content can now appear in Story waves instead of filling the map with every marker at once.
- Quiet aftermaths return control to the player so reactions and environmental changes can be noticed before the next major objective.
- Phase changes never reduce an already unlocked quest wave.

## Chapter 1 — Beginning the Journey
- Tournament Road now tracks a light journey arc from the Training Grounds to the Tournament Outskirts.
- The chapter remains the shortest and simplest introduction; no new mandatory errands were added.
- Existing route choice, transport rescue, roadside challenge, and technique interactions remain the chapter’s main variety.

## Chapter 2 — Tournament Day
- First-time players now learn the festival before the bracket begins.
- Registration requires visiting at least three districts, including Central Plaza, and meeting two people.
- The hub objective clearly reports orientation progress.
- Optional activities unlock in waves as the festival develops instead of appearing as one giant checklist.
- Tournament intermissions include a short player-controlled aftermath for crowd, fighter, and worker reactions.
- Existing Festival Photo Stand, Wade race, Bark ring investigation, bracket-card activity, technique challenges, and tournament fights remain intact.

## Chapter 3 — Something Under the Ring
- The player first experiences the closed tournament grounds as a changed version of Chapter 2.
- The first reliable investigation lead opens after visiting the Main Arena and at least one other district.
- Optional investigations appear in three waves as the mystery escalates.
- Discovering Project Hollow creates a controlled aftermath before the facility sequence continues.
- The chapter still avoids repeated plaza laps through its consolidated evidence systems.

## Chapter 4 — Echo Region
- Rrvvfo now reaches Echo Village before Bark and Wade arrive.
- The player learns how the village works by inspecting the Resonance Wall and Water Channel.
- Bark and Wade’s arrival begins only after both landmarks are understood.
- The completed village defense creates a quiet recovery period with control retained.
- Existing Echo Village visual recovery, Echo Chime Jam, team missions, Ryuzankaro branch, mountain route, Hollow Watcher, and floating-lookout Object Swap remain intact.
- Older saves that already reached Bark and Wade automatically receive the two landmark checks so progression cannot be trapped.

## Presentation
- Added phase-aware objective accents and quest-journal pacing cards.
- Arrival, crisis, aftermath, and departure use different visual emphasis without covering gameplay.
- Mobile layouts retain compact spacing.

## Compatibility
- This is a complete cumulative build; older packages are not required.
- Save export schema remains **268**.
- Existing Chapter 4 saves normalize through the new orientation state.
