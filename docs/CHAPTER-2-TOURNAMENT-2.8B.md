# Chapter 2 — Definitely Not the World Tournament

## Hub opening

Rrvvfo explores the local tournament grounds with Sage. After Rrvvfo talks to two locals, Sage says he needs to check something and disappears behind a tent despite there being nowhere to go.

## First brawl and Training Levels

A practice-ring fighter says Sage arranged a test before disappearing. Winning introduces Story Training Levels through Sage's Combat Manual. The system grants small Story-only benefits and does not affect Versus or Arena modes.

## Bark, Wade, and optional encounters

Bark and Wade arrive after the first brawl. Bark offers an optional spar before the tournament. Two optional grunts can also challenge Rrvvfo. The player may fight or attempt a short escape QTE.

## Tournament bracket

1. Rrvvfo versus a qualifier fighter.
2. Rrvvfo versus a second bracket fighter.
3. Bark versus Pouki, with Pouki winning easily.
4. Rrvvfo versus Wade.
5. Plouke defeats Pouki on the opposite side of the bracket.
6. Rrvvfo versus Plouke in the final.

## Final match

Rrvvfo begins confidently but becomes more exhausted as the fight continues. The story prevents him from defeating Plouke. Rrvvfo attempts Fire Awakening, cannot sustain it, and enters an interactive beam clash. The clash is programmed to end in defeat, although the player's resistance changes Rrvvfo's final line.

Plouke then reveals himself as Sage, who entered in disguise to train Rrvvfo while making him believe Sage was absent.
