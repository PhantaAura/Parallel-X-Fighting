# Prototype 2.7B.1 — Road Rebuild

This correction rebuilds the short Chapter 1 road around real field obstacles and more story activity.

- Fixes Rrvvfo facing opposite his movement in hub mode.
- Makes the river permanently impassable instead of turning into walkable ground after progression.
- Adds a real highlighted rock on the far bank and makes Object Swap trade Rrvvfo's position with that rock.
- Removes the misleading walkable bridge span and draws only broken bridge remains.
- Adds a three-flag dojo warm-up loop before Rrvvfo can leave.
- Adds a mandatory roadside worker scene and Fire Blast roadblock.
- Adds a tournament checkpoint conversation and stadium reveal scene.
- Expands the road population from four roaming NPCs to seven.
- Adds a Fire Blast field page to the Combat Manual.

The previous 8–12 minute estimate was not supported by the actual build. This patch expands the route, but the playtime should be judged from a real run rather than another estimate.
