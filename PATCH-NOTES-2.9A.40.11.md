# Prototype 2.9A.40.11 — Chapters 5–8

## New playable story content

- Added the rest of Rrvvfo’s eight-chapter route as regular chapters. Chapter Select and automatic Continue both unlock each chapter in order.
- Chapter 5 sends Rrvvfo to three different Red bases: combat observation, power replication, and the Hollow Archive. It ends with Hollow Mimic and the biological clone reveal.
- The Organization of the Red is the faction. “Project Hollow” remains the name of its older cloning program inside the Archive.
- Rrvvfo invents the unstable Shots of Agony prototype in Chapter 5. The Chapter 3 blue-clone lesson sets up how he thinks about independent copies; the new technique is unlocked through the Story save.
- The Sage’s one-use life-support pulse is established in Chapter 5 and pays off during Chapter 7.
- Chapter 6 gives the clone real copied memories, an Echo Village identity mix-up, a mirror duel, and the Emerald theft that begins the network activation.
- Chapter 7 makes Rrvvfo’s power sacrifice the turning point. The clone joins the team by choice after the Red turns on him.
- Chapter 8 gives Bark and Wade separate jobs in the central-facility attack, ends with a three-phase Leader fight and a timing-assisted Double Solar Wave, and closes with the Co-Leader escaping.

## Route and quality-of-life updates

- Chapters 5–8 save objective checkpoints, awards, choices, and completed-mission state in the existing Story save schema (268).
- Added recent objective history to the new chapters, with the same saved NOW/DONE journal used by Chapters 2–4.
- Added safe fight retries, Story Assist after repeated losses, chapter replay, confirmed chapter restart, and temporary playtest jumps for Chapters 5–8.
- Added one optional three-step side quest to each new chapter: secure field evidence, help Echo Village, rescue relay workers, and evacuate the Red facility. Each task has choice-driven approaches, saved step progress, and a one-time XP/item reward; skipping never blocks the main story.
- Added chapter pacing targets: Chapter 6 is the shortest new chapter, Chapter 7 gives the sacrifice room to land, and Chapter 8 carries the longest new sequence.
- Added chapter-specific afterglow, save reliability checks, route status, and full-route completion at 8/8.
- Bumped the GitHub Pages cache token to `29a411-orgred-chapters-20261122`.
