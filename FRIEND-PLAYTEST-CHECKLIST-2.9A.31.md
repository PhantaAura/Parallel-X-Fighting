# Friend Playtest Checklist — Prototype 2.9A.31

## Responsiveness
- Press Light, Heavy, or Launcher during the last moments of an attack and verify it begins after recovery instead of disappearing.
- Confirm buffered attacks do not skip recovery or create infinite strings.

## Fighter identity
- Compare Rrvvfo, Revvfo, Wade, and Bark dash cancellation, hit weight, landing effects, and victory presentation.
- Confirm Wade feels snappy and Bark feels heavy without either becoming unfair.

## Replay flow
- Use Instant Rematch repeatedly.
- Use Random Rematch and confirm fighters never mirror and the stage is playable.
- Complete a Story fight and confirm Random Rematch is not offered there.

## Training
- Open Fighter Identity Guide.
- Verify the current fighter card is highlighted and mobile layout remains readable.

## Regression
- Run the complete smoke suite.
- Test Story fights, Pursuit, Focus Recovery, ring-outs, Training reset, controller, keyboard, and touch.
