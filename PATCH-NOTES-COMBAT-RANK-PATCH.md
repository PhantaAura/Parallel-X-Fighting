# Combat Rank Patch — Prototype 2.9A.38

Small unnumbered patch requested before the next major update.

## Universal fight grades

Every completed player-controlled fight now uses the full six-rank ladder:

- S — 90–100
- A — 75–89
- B — 60–74
- C — 45–59
- D — 25–44
- E — 0–24

Arena/VS results show the rank on the main result card. Story fights show a short non-blocking Fight Rank card after the encounter, including the Chapter 1 road fight, Chapter 2 tournament fights/final, Chapter 3 encounters, and Chapter 4 battles/bosses.

Ranks are based on the existing combat-mastery score: victory, combo quality, parries, guard breaks, pursuit finishers, signature mechanics, stage conversions, action variety, damage taken, and clean wins.

The patch keeps save schema 268 and remains Prototype 2.9A.38.
