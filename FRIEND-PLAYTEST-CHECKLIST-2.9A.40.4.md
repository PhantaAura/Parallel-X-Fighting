# Friend Playtest Checklist — Prototype 2.9A.40.4

The goal is not only “doors work.” Check whether buildings feel like believable parts of the RPG world.

## Chapter 2

- Walk into Tournament Administration, the Medical Center, and Fighter Backstage.
- Confirm the exterior walls are solid and Rrvvfo cannot walk through the buildings.
- Exit each building and confirm you return to the correct district/door.
- Open the map inside: it should show the local interior, not the whole tournament region.

## Chapter 3

- Enter the Medical Center before the medical lead is ready. The building should still be enterable, but the worker should not skip the required orientation pacing.
- When the medical lead becomes active, speak to the worker inside the clinic.
- Return for the required medical-worker revisit inside the same clinic.
- Complete Medical Follow-Up by speaking to that same indoor NPC. There should be no duplicate outdoor medical-worker marker and finishing the quest should leave you inside the clinic.
- Revisit Tournament Administration and Fighter Backstage after hours and check whether they feel like the same Chapter 2 locations at a different time.

## Chapter 4

- Enter the Old Apothecary and both released Echo homes.
- Try walking through their exterior walls: collision should stop you.
- Knock/check the intentionally locked Echo doors and make sure the short Rrvvfo response communicates that they are background buildings.
- Confirm the old man is visible outside rather than hidden inside the apothecary shell.
- Check interior NPC lines before/after village defense where possible.

## Persistence

- Enter several interiors, leave the chapter or reload, then inspect the Travel Journal/Map state.
- Confirm discovered interiors remain remembered.
- Confirm no existing Chapter 1–4 Story progress, field skills, builds, or shortcuts are lost.

## Feel questions

- Did entering a building feel immediate enough, or did it interrupt exploration?
- Did any room feel pointlessly large or empty?
- Did you naturally want to check doors?
- Were locked/background doors understandable without a giant UI warning?
- Did the tournament and Echo buildings visually fit their regions?
