# Friend Playtest — Prototype 2.9A.38

The important question is not “does the menu work?” It is: **does mastery make you want one more fight?**

## Fresh player
1. Open Extras → **MASTERY & RECORDS** before doing any mastery trial.
2. Confirm the archive is understandable even with zero progress.
3. Enter Arena Training and choose a trial that sounds fun without being told which one is easiest.
4. Complete it and return to Mastery & Records.
5. Confirm the medal/reward feels noticeable and understandable.

## Fighter identity
Try at least two:
- Rrvvfo — Object Swap → close hit.
- Revvfo — keep close pressure for three actions.
- Wade — dash through an active strike.
- Bark — absorb with armor and punish.

Ask: did the challenge teach what makes that fighter special, or did it just feel like homework?

## Real fight
1. Play an Arena/CPU match using the Arena engine.
2. Try to naturally use a parry, guard break, pursuit, and fighter signature.
3. Finish the match.
4. Read the C/B/A/S mastery result.
5. Rematch once because you want to improve the grade — not because the checklist told you to.

## No-grind check
Repeat an already-completed Training mastery challenge. The saved challenge may improve to a better grade, but it must not repeatedly farm the first-clear mastery reward.

## Fun questions
- Did the rank make you understand what you did well?
- Did you want to retry an A to get S?
- Was any challenge annoying enough that you would rather ignore the whole system?
- Did the reward feel like a cool record of the adventure or just another number?
- Which fighter’s identity challenge was the most fun?
- Would you willingly open Adventure Records again after another chapter?
