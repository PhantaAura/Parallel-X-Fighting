# Living Road 2.7B.1

## Required Chapter 1 route

1. Complete the three training flags around the dojo.
2. Leave through the east training gate.
3. Reach the broken river.
4. Object Swap with the physical rock on the far bank.
5. Help the road worker clear the fallen practice log with Fire Blast.
6. Open the four-target gate with Shots of Agony.
7. Fight or escape the roaming challenger.
8. Pass the tournament checkpoint.
9. Reveal the false roadblock with Lens of Truth.
10. Reach the stadium reveal.

The river collision remains active for the entire route. Object Swap crosses it; completing the story does not make the water walkable.
