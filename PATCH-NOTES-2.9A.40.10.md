# Prototype 2.9A.40.10 — Pacing & Objective QoL

Cumulative Legacy Pages update based on 2.9A.40.9. Save schema remains **268**.

## Remake QoL brought into Legacy
- Added optional **Hold to Advance Dialogue** in Gameplay settings. It requires a short hold on keyboard, controller, mouse, or touch; dialogue choices still need a fresh press.
- Added a chapter-filtered objective history to Chapters 2–4. Open it with the **OBJECTIVES** button or **J**; it keeps recent goals across refreshes and marks the current item as **NOW**.
- Included objective history in save export and reset behavior.

## Pacing changes
- Chapter 2's festival orientation now asks for two districts and one conversation instead of three districts and two conversations. The required central plaza visit and tournament preparation remain.
- Chapter 4 now requires two lift components; the third component remains an optional pickup. This trims one repeated cavern fetch while preserving all three elemental seals, party beats, village defense, and the mountain finale.
- Revised experience targets: Chapter 2 **65–85 min**, Chapter 3 **45–65 min**, Chapter 4 **75–95 min**. Chapter 4 remains the longest released chapter.
