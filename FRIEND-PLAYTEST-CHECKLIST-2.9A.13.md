# Prototype 2.9A.13 Friend Playtest

## Progression

- Start with a fresh save and confirm Arena, VS CPU, and 2 Player are absent.
- Complete or load Chapters 1–3 and confirm those modes are still absent.
- Confirm Training remains available.

## Hub camera

- Dash repeatedly across the Chapter 1 road and confirm Rrvvfo remains centered.
- Use Object Swap at the river and confirm the camera snaps to the new position.
- Explore the far edges of the Chapter 2 tournament hub and use Wade’s shortcuts.
- Explore the Chapter 3 investigation hub and watch for scenery blocking Rrvvfo.

## Fighters

- Check idle, run, jump, attacks, hurt, knockdown, victory, and special moves for Rrvvfo, Revvfo, Bark, Wade, and the Sage.
- Report any surviving printed text, panel line, neighboring body part, clipped body, detached effect, or sudden character-design change.

## Tangai Dojo

- Enter Arena Training and inspect the 3D Dojo from both fighter sides.
- Enter a Classic battle in Tangai Dojo after using a development/test save.
- Check mobile performance around the new scenery.

## Known audit items not repaired in this build

- The Story route progress display can still reach 100% after Chapter 3.
- Chapter 2 hub actor animation speed can vary by display refresh rate.
- The Sage’s clean atlas reuses a small set of authored poses.
- Hub cameras do not yet handle scenery occlusion.
