# Friend Playtest Checklist — Prototype 2.9A.30

## Stage Select

- Confirm Tangai Dojo, Global Tournament, Resonance Facility, Echo Caverns, and Mountain Path are selectable.
- Confirm each card shows size, boundary type, archetype, tags, description, recommendation, and dimensions.
- Confirm selecting every stage loads the correct arena and camera.

## Global Tournament

- Confirm the ring remains open and free of obstacles.
- Push an opponent toward every edge and confirm the boundary is easy to read.
- Trigger strong edge pressure and listen for crowd/announcer reactions.
- Trigger a ring-out and confirm wall splat never replaces it.
- Confirm both fighters respawn correctly after a ring-out.

## Walled stages

- Trigger wall splats in the Dojo, Facility, and Echo Caverns.
- Confirm each location has a distinct impact color/effect.
- Confirm one wall splat per combo and no camera lock after impact.

## Focus Recovery

- Take damage and confirm a gray segment appears beside current health.
- Hold Block + Charge while standing still and confirm healing waits through the startup.
- Confirm healing restores only gray health, spends Energy, and stops at the gray-health limit.
- Release recovery and confirm the fighter has a brief vulnerable recovery period.
- Get hit while recovering and confirm it interrupts immediately.
- Try moving, attacking, dashing, jumping, or pursuing while recovering and confirm the channel ends or the action is blocked.
- Confirm no recovery is possible without gray health or enough Energy.

## CPU and Training

- Complete the Focus Recovery Training trial.
- Test all four dummy recovery behaviors.
- Confirm the recovery dummy starts with visible gray health after reset.
- Confirm Easy rarely recovers and harder CPUs use recovery only after earning distance.
- Rush a recovering CPU and confirm it stops or gets punished instead of ignoring the approach.

## Mobile

- Test landscape on the iPhone Home Screen version.
- Confirm the gray health segment remains readable.
- Confirm the Focus Recovery cue does not cover health, Energy, touch controls, or the ability row.
- Hold Block + Charge with two fingers and confirm both inputs remain active.
- Rotate, pause, reset, and resume without leaving a stuck recovery cue.

## Cleanup

- Reset Training during startup and active recovery.
- End a round while either fighter is recovering.
- Pause and exit during recovery.
- Confirm gray health, release recovery, sounds, cues, and CPU intent reset correctly.
