# Prototype 2.7A — Continuous Chapter Overhaul Foundation

This is the first implementation phase of the Story Mode overhaul.

## Replaces Mission Select with continuous chapters

- First-time selection of Rrvvfo begins the route immediately.
- Mission 0, Mission 1, and Mission 2 remain internal save checkpoints.
- The player experiences them as two connected chapters.
- Completing one checkpoint automatically continues to the next section.
- Returning later opens a route home with Continue Story and Chapter Select.

## Persistent Combat Manual

- Sage still gives Rrvvfo the manual during Chapter 1.
- The manual stays available from the Rrvvfo route and tournament screen.
- New pages unlock when later mechanics are discovered.
- Rrvvfo reacts to the first discoveries with the planned escalating comments.
- Existing save data remains compatible.

## Chapter 2 ending

The tournament arrival now ends with a clear current-content notice explaining that tournament matches, roaming challengers, side stories, and living 3D hubs continue in later overhaul phases.

## Included compatibility

The Prototype 2.6.5 sprite-on-top dialogue files are bundled so this patch can safely install whether the previous dialogue merge was already installed or not.

## Not included yet

This foundation does not yet replace the current tournament checklist with the fully explorable 3D living hub. That is the next major phase after the route and manual architecture are stable.
