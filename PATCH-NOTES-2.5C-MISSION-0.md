# Prototype 2.5C — Rrvvfo Mission 0: No Maximums

## Playable story mission

Rrvvfo's Lost Year route now begins with **Mission 0: No Maximums**.

The Sage trains Rrvvfo in the Sage Training Field and asks him to concentrate energy into his hand by remembering Alt's Season 1 technique. The opening dialogue establishes the first energy copy, the four-copy limit, and the Sage's response: **there are no maximums**.

## Mission rules

- The Sage is a story-only mentor opponent, not a selectable roster fighter.
- Direct attacks cannot damage him during this mission.
- He uses a visible prediction dodge rather than invisible invulnerability.
- After three failed direct attacks, the Shots training objective unlocks.
- Shots of Agony develops through four attempts: 1 clone, 2 clones, 3 clones, then 4 clones.
- The Sage dodges the first three incomplete volleys.
- The four-clone volley surrounds him and catches him outside his prediction window.
- The mission ends when the complete volley lands. The player does not defeat or knock out the Sage.

## Story progression

Completing the mission saves:

- `rrvvfo-00` as completed
- `shotsOfAgony` as a story unlock
- 50% progress for the currently planned two-mission Rrvvfo route
