# Friend Playtest Checklist — Prototype 2.9A.7

The goal is to catch any moment where a casual player becomes confused, bored, feels cheated, or cannot work out what to do next.

## First ten minutes

1. Start with **Arena Training** from the main menu.
2. Confirm M1/J Light, M2/L Block, C Charge, U Grab, R Breaker, Q Counter, and E Interact.
3. On controller, use D-pad Left/Right to select an ability and ZR/RT/R2 to activate it. Selecting must not move the fighter.
4. Switch between keyboard/mouse and controller. Prompts should update immediately.
5. Check that the basic Training panel is understandable without opening Advanced 2D Lab.

## Quick Battle

6. Open VS CPU and play **Quick Battle** on Easy.
7. Both health bars must start at 100.
8. The match must end after one KO.
9. Try the full first-to-three option afterward and check that only the defeated fighter respawns between KOs.
10. Confirm the CPU feels easier through behavior—not hidden extra health.

## Roster and setup

11. Only Rrvvfo, Revvfo, Wade, Bark, and The Sage should be selectable.
12. Random must stay inside that group.
13. Coming Soon fighters should be clearly disabled rather than looking broken.
14. The normal setup should feel simple; technical controls should stay under Advanced Settings.

## Lens and defense

15. Use Lens at low mastery. Its warning may be broad, but it must match the opponent’s real behavior.
16. Confirm Lens costs 60 Energy and 25 HP at zero mastery and cannot reduce the user below 1 HP.
17. Hold Block in Arena until the warning appears. The warning must clearly happen before guard fatigue disables Block.
18. Try Combo Breaker while stunned and verify the Energy cost is understandable.

## Chapter 1 road

19. MOVE, JUMP, and DASH flags must require three different actions.
20. Talk to an NPC with Interact. Light Attack must not be required for dialogue.
21. Check that Object Swap, Fire Blast, Shots of Agony, and Lens prompts match the active device.
22. New Sage’s Manual pages should appear as compact notifications, not repeated full-screen interruptions.
23. Fail the Run sequence once and retry it.
24. A second failure must start a first-to-1 fight with 100 HP on both sides.

## Quit-risk report

Immediately write down any moment where you:

- Did not know the objective or input.
- Could not tell why damage happened.
- Felt an encounter lasted too long.
- Thought the CPU was cheating.
- Could not leave, restart, or return from a screen.
- Saw a prompt for the wrong device.
- Felt the camera, hit feedback, music, or menus became annoying.
