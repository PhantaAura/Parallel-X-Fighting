# Friend Playtest Checklist — Prototype 2.9A.40

Do not explain where secrets or optional goals are unless the player is genuinely stuck. The most useful observation is what they choose to do without being told.

## Core question

After 20–30 minutes, ask only: **“What do you want to do next?”**

Good signs:
- They want another fight without Story forcing one.
- They retry a rank voluntarily.
- They change Rrvvfo's build to test it.
- They investigate a path/NPC/object because it looks interesting.
- They notice that chapters play differently.

Bad signs:
- They only follow objective markers.
- They ask how long until the next cutscene/fight repeatedly.
- They ignore every optional interaction because rewards feel meaningless.
- Combat devolves into repeating one attack.

- [ ] Broken river/Object Swap traversal is readable.
