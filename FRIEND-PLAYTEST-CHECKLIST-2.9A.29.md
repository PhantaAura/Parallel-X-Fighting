# Friend Playtest Checklist — 2.9A.29

## Pursuit basics

- Land Launcher, press Dash during the prompt, and confirm the chase reaches the opponent.
- Press Light during the chase and confirm it activates on arrival.
- Press Heavy during the chase and confirm the direct finisher activates on arrival.
- Use Launcher → Dash → Light → Heavy and confirm the linked finisher is reliable.
- Let the chase expire without attacking and confirm the fighter returns to normal control.

## Defensive tech

- Have the CPU launch and pursue you in the Pursuit Escape trial.
- Press Dash during the incoming-pursuit prompt.
- Confirm 18 Energy is spent, the chase cancels, and control returns immediately.
- Try with less than 18 Energy and confirm the escape does not activate.

## Wall and ground reactions

- In a non-ring-out arena, hit an opponent near a wall with Heavy or Light 3 and confirm one wall splat.
- Continue the combo and confirm a second wall splat does not occur.
- Land Air Heavy or Pursuit Heavy and confirm one ground bounce.
- Continue the combo and confirm a second ground bounce does not occur.
- In the Chapter 2 tournament ring-out arena, confirm wall splats do not interfere with ring-out rules.

## Fighter identity

- Rrvvfo and Phanta should visibly sidestep during their feint dashes.
- Revvfo should blink forward when beginning pursuit.
- Wade should have the fastest dash cadence and chase speed.
- Bark should feel slower but resist grounded knockback and armor through part of his step.
- Creed should have a longer evasive dash window.

## Readability and regression

- Prompts should never cover health, energy, guard, touch controls, or Story objectives.
- The pursuit camera should keep both fighters visible and restore normal framing afterward.
- Training reset must clear pursuit, wall-splat, and ground-bounce state.
- Story fights, VS CPU, 2 Player, Arena, and Training must preserve the same controls.
- Complete one normal three-KO match and confirm rounds, results, pause, and rematch still work.
