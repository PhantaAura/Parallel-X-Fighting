# Friend Playtest Checklist — 2.9A.29.1

## Feel and timing

- Land Launcher → Dash and confirm pursuit looks and sounds different from a normal dash.
- Complete Launcher → Dash → Light → Heavy and confirm the Heavy finisher feels stronger without freezing too long.
- Confirm the lock-on marker is brief and both fighters remain visible.
- Miss a pursuit and confirm the camera, music, prompt, and controls return normally.
- Compare Rrvvfo, Revvfo, Wade, Bark, Phanta, and Creed; each movement identity should be noticeable without changing damage.

## Pursuit Tech

- With exactly 15 Energy, press Dash while being pursued and confirm the Tech succeeds.
- With less than 15 Energy, confirm it fails, spends nothing, and does not trap the input.
- Confirm a successful Tech gives the defender a real chance to punish.
- Attempt another Tech immediately and confirm the cooldown prevents repeated escapes.
- Compare Easy, Normal, and Hard CPU behavior; higher difficulty should be smarter, not able to Tech every route.

## Prompts and mobile

- Test Pursuit Prompts on Full, Minimal, and Off. Combat behavior must remain identical.
- On Full, confirm ATTACK READY appears at the legal buffered-attack timing.
- On iPhone, confirm the prompt never covers health, Energy, Guard, the hotbar, or touch buttons.
- Confirm the Dash touch target becomes easier to hit during pursuit and returns to normal afterward.
- Pause during pursuit and confirm all temporary prompts and pulses disappear.

## Training

- Complete Ideal Pursuit Combo and receive a Good, Great, or Perfect grade.
- Complete Pursuit Pressure using a Tech and punish.
- Test all four Pursuit Defense dummy settings.
- Confirm Wall Splat and Ground Bounce counters never exceed 1/1 inside one combo.
- Use Reset Combat State during every pursuit phase and confirm normal control and camera return immediately.

## Reactions and cleanup

- Trigger wall splat and ground bounce in a non-ring-out arena and confirm their effects and sounds are distinct.
- In the Chapter 2 tournament arena, confirm wall splat never replaces ring-out behavior.
- Reset Training, finish a round, rematch, exit to menu, and enter a Story fight after pursuit; no chase, camera, buffer, invulnerability, or prompt state should remain.
- Complete a normal three-KO match and confirm results, pause, rematch, and exit still work.
