# Parallels X: Clash of Souls — Prototype 2.5A

## Lost Year Foundation

This checkpoint starts the new campaign structure without replacing or rewriting the existing combat systems.

### Added

- New main-menu entry: **Story Mode — The Lost Year**
- New full-screen Lost Year character-story selector
- Eight visible parallel routes:
  - Rrvvfo — Restless Flame
  - Alt & Rover — For the Firstborn
  - Bark — Keeper of Peace
  - Wade — Ten Minutes Around the World
  - Robert — White Survivor
  - The Oddballs — Three Tiny Problems
  - Rev & Metal — The Hidden Side
  - Final Story — The Lost Year
- Rrvvfo route available as the first foundation route
- Mission-select and briefing foundation for **Mission 1: No Peace**
- Local save foundation for selected route, viewed briefings, and future mission completion
- Locked routes remain visible so the full campaign structure can be planned in-game
- Canon clarification in the route descriptions: Rev and Metal are separate from Alt and Rover after Season 1
- Oddballs route correctly frames them as causing havoc and mayhem for fun

### Global Tournament enlargement

Tangai Dojo remains unchanged.

Global Tournament is now a genuinely large arena:

- Playable bounds increased from `940 × 600` to `1500 × 900`
- Floor and ring geometry enlarged to match the playable space
- Spawn points moved much farther apart
- Projectile cleanup padding increased
- Camera distance, focus range, fog, and zoom limits retuned
- Crowd, stands, scoreboard, lamps, ropes, and posts spread out to match the larger ring
- Stage-specific CPU wall-awareness margin added

The Global Tournament now has about 3.9 times Tangai Dojo's playable floor area.

### Intentionally not included yet

- No story dialogue is locked in this checkpoint
- Mission 1 combat is not playable yet
- Alt, Bark, Wade, Robert, the Oddballs, Rev, and Metal do not receive new arena movesets in this checkpoint
- No Asrylyte Zone work
- No merge to `main` yet

- Main menu: `Prototype 2.5A — Lost Year Foundation`
- Arena badge: `PROTOTYPE 2.5A • LOST YEAR FOUNDATION`
