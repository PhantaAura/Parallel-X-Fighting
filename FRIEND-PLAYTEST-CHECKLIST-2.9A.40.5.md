# Friend Playtest Checklist — Prototype 2.9A.40.5

The question for this build is not only “does it work?” It is “does the same world feel more expressive and satisfying?”

## 1. Character charm
- Play the Chapter 2 hub through at least one tournament round.
- Notice whether the short banter feels natural or annoying.
- In Chapter 4, reach Bark/Wade reunion, village defense, and the solo mountain split.
- Check that banter does not repeat after reload/revisit.

## 2. World Delight
- Finish Chapter 1's cliff route.
- Use a Wade shortcut in Chapter 2.
- Open the Chapter 3 night service shortcut.
- In Chapter 4, activate the Old Water Lift and the Old Apothecary Passage if available.
- Find the Echo overlook without relying on a far-away marker.
- Confirm discoveries feel optional rather than like mandatory quests.

## 3. Learned-by-doing
- Earn/re-earn a field technique on a fresh/appropriate save.
- Check the mastery card, sound, and character reaction.
- Confirm the presentation is memorable but brief.

## 4. Combat feel
- Land Flow Cancel several times.
- Perfect-parry a real opponent.
- Land a pursuit finisher.
- Interrupt a Chapter 4 Support cast.
- Check that the effects feel stronger without making the camera uncomfortable.

## 5. Chapter 4 squad readability
- Let Bark or Wade drop below roughly 30% HP.
- If possible, let one ally get knocked down.
- Win a village defense with all three standing.
- Check whether you understand party danger without staring at the HUD.

## 6. Hollow Watcher
- Reach all three scan phases.
- Confirm each phase transition feels different.
- Confirm the boss still rewards varying action/range/route rather than becoming a cutscene-heavy fight.
