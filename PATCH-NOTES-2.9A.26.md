# Prototype 2.9A.26 — Combat Feel, Training & Evidence Pass

## Combat feel and readability

- Added independent **Impact Freeze / Hitstop** accessibility control: Full, Reduced, or Off.
- Existing Camera Shake remains independently adjustable.
- Heavy hits, launchers, perfect parries, and guard breaks use clearer timing feedback without forcing strong motion effects on every player.
- Energy bars gain near-ready and full-energy cues.
- Guard bars gain low and critical cues.
- Character movement and impacts use distinct visual accents:
  - Rrvvfo: fire/orange trails
  - Revvfo: Astrylte-purple distortion
  - Wade: lightning/cyan afterimages
  - Bark: earth-colored dust and weight

## Training expansion

- Added defensive dummy settings:
  - Stand
  - Always Block
  - Perfect-Block Attempts
  - Counter After Hit
  - Air-Tech Practice
  - Random Defense
  - Full CPU
- Added focused Training trials:
  - Perfect Parry
  - Energy Discipline
  - Launcher → Pursuit → Follow-up
  - Guard Break → Grab
  - Unpredictable Route
- Added live trial goals, progress, completion state, reset behavior, and event tracking.

## Chapter 3 evidence board

- Replaced the generic quest presentation with a CASE BOARD.
- The board groups the investigation into current deduction, working theory, unresolved contradictions, main threads, and collected evidence.
- Existing Chapter 3 dialogue, Strange Man sequence, facility route, fights, teleporter ending, optional quests, and key items are preserved.

## Chapter 4 combat roles and swarm readability

- Project Hollow enemies now receive readable roles with different stat profiles and visual identities.
- Added Scout, Striker, Raider, Heavy, Commander, Watcher, and Boss roles.
- Added enemy-role cards, team-support status, and wave banners during multi-enemy encounters.
- Bark and Wade support status remains visible during team battles without covering the core combat HUD.

## Preserved systems

- 2.9A.25.1 Chapter Select layout and Chapter 4 Replay/Restart.
- 2.9A.25 hub collision, team fights, swarms, Hollow Watcher adaptation, chapter results, and reward soft caps.
- Chapters 1–4 story content and eight-chapter route planning.
