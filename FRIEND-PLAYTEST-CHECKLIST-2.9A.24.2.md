# Friend Playtest Checklist — 2.9A.24.2

## Mac mouse / trackpad

- Move the pointer over the game without clicking: the camera must not move.
- Click a Story menu, dialogue choice, Map, Journal, or pause button: the camera must not move.
- Hold the primary click on the 3D world and drag: the camera should rotate smoothly.
- Release the click: camera movement must stop immediately.
- Leave the camera at an angle for several seconds: it should keep that angle.

## Settings

- Open Settings → Gameplay.
- Set Hub Free Camera to Off: mouse/trackpad and right-stick look should stop and the view should return toward default.
- Turn it back to Click and Drag.
- Test low and high Camera Look Sensitivity.
- Reload the page and confirm both choices persist.

## Controller

- Right stick rotates the camera only during exploration.
- Starting a fight restores the fixed fight camera.
- Returning to the hub restores exploration camera control.

## Regression

- Menus remain clickable.
- Left-click attacks still work during fights.
- Chapters 1–4 can still enter and exit combat.
- Rrvvfo’s sprite remains visible after fights.
