# Prototype 2.9A.10 Friend Playtest Checklist

## Before testing

- Force-refresh the site with **Command + Shift + R** on Mac or **Ctrl + Shift + R** on Windows.
- Confirm the main menu says **Prototype 2.9A.10 — Living Tournament Hub**.
- Start Rrvvfo’s Story and continue to Chapter 2.

## Arrival and navigation

- Confirm the announcer clearly starts **The Lost Bracket**.
- Recover the three cards from the fan, food vendor, and old competitor.
- Check that district titles appear while moving through the grounds.
- Report any path that looks open but cannot be crossed.
- Report any district that still feels empty or has no reason to visit.

## Mandatory quest flow

- Finish the bracket quest and confirm Sage disappears normally.
- Complete the first practice brawl and meet Bark and Wade.
- Start **Wade’s Shortcut** and pass all five checkpoints.
- Confirm finishing the route works even when Wade’s target time is not beaten.
- Test all three Wade shortcut signs after the race.
- Start **The Cracked Ring**, inspect all three supports, and find the saboteur.
- Confirm the saboteur fight teaches the visible ring-out sequence and can be left after a loss.
- Verify tournament registration remains locked only until Bracket, Wade’s Shortcut, and Bark’s Ring are complete.

## Tournament intermissions

- Confirm the hub reopens after the qualifier.
- Get the first Plouke clue from the old competitor.
- Confirm the hub reopens after the quarterfinal and get the worker’s clue.
- Watch Bark versus Pouki, return to the hub, and get Bark’s clue.
- Beat Wade, return to the hub, and get Wade’s final clue.
- Try using the bracket board before collecting the required clue; it should point toward the correct source.
- Confirm the bracket board starts the next event after the clue is found.

## Optional side quests

None of these should block tournament progress.

- **Controlled Flame:** complete three orders and verify the chosen meal activates in the next official fight.
- Lose and retry that fight; the meal should remain active on the retry without being consumed twice.
- **Fake Champion:** test both Lens and fight routes across separate saves when possible.
- **Wade’s Biggest Fan:** confirm talking to Wade completes the objective and grants +6 Story HP.
- **Dummy on the Loose:** confirm the reward adds permanent Story Defense.
- **Missing Prize Envelope:** catch the moving cart, receive 80 coins, then buy a discounted 20-coin meal after the current meal is gone.
- **One Match Anyway:** choose Power or Speed and confirm the selected stat increases.
- Confirm undiscovered side quests are not displayed as a giant mandatory checklist.

## RPG and battle checks

- Open the Story menu before and after a permanent stat reward.
- Confirm HP, Power, Defense, Speed, or Focus changes correctly.
- Confirm the same bonus affects the following Story fight.
- Verify later tournament opponents still scale fairly with Rrvvfo’s level.
- Lose an official tournament fight twice and confirm Story Assist appears.
- Confirm the Run button remains visible during official fights and Rrvvfo refuses to forfeit.

## Device and presentation checks

- Test keyboard and mouse: E to interact, M for Manual, T for tracker, Escape for Story menu.
- Test a controller and confirm prompts match the selected Nintendo, Xbox, or PlayStation style.
- Test landscape phone controls and report any quest toast, race HUD, prompt, or flame-game overlap.
- Listen for audio overlap when entering and leaving fights.
- Report frame drops caused by crowds, birds, carts, signs, or the larger hub paths.

## Most useful feedback

Record where you became lost, bored, unable to interact, or unsure whether an activity was mandatory. For each problem, include:

- Area or quest name
- Input device
- What the objective said
- What you expected to happen
- What actually happened
- Screenshot or short recording when possible
