PARALLELS X
CHAPTER 1 — REWRITTEN DIALOGUE
Safe-Keeping Draft

==================================================
PART 1 — NO MAXIMUMS
==================================================

Context:
Rrvvfo has recently started training with the Sage. He doubts that the Sage is a real legendary master and keeps calling him a pervert, which the Sage hates.

SAGE:
“Eh, focus your energy into your hands or something. I’m getting bored. I have places to be.”

RRVVFO:
“Maybe this would be easier if you actually taught me stuff. I can already imagine what places a perv like you has to go.”

SAGE:
“HEY! I told you to stop calling me that!”

[Rrvvfo concentrates and creates his first energy copy.]

SAGE:
“Took you long enough. Now get four out. Let’s see if you still have any fighting spirit in you.”

[Rrvvfo creates four copies.]

RRVVFO:
“This is my max capacity.”

SAGE:
“Eh, now you’ve done something. Summon them again and make each one attack me with a specific action.”

[The training fight begins. Rrvvfo coordinates the four copies and catches the Sage.]

RRVVFO:
“Caught ya.”

SAGE:
“Alright, training’s over. I’ve got important stuff to do.”

RRVVFO:
“Wait—no! We just got started!”

[The Sage disappears.]

RRVVFO, under his breath:
“You damn perv.”

[Rrvvfo looks at the remaining energy from the technique.]

RRVVFO:
“I’ll name this attack... Shots of Agony.”
