# Friend Playtest Checklist — Prototype 2.9A.8

This test is about whether the new combat feels fast, understandable, fair, and different between fighters. Record anything that feels confusing or makes you stop having fun.

## First five minutes — movement

1. Open **Arena Training** and move in every depth direction.
2. Run, stop, reverse direction, jump, ground dash, and air dash.
3. Confirm the fighter feels fast without becoming hard to control.
4. Dash into Light, Heavy, Launcher, and Jump.
5. Stand slightly out of depth alignment and attack. Modest correction is expected, but attacks should not pull across an unreasonable distance.
6. Confirm the ground shadows and movement trails make both fighters’ positions understandable.

## Character identity

Play briefly as each finished fighter:

7. **Rrvvfo:** confirm the normals feel balanced rather than fastest or strongest.
8. **Revvfo:** confirm his pressure and pursuit feel more aggressive than Rrvvfo’s.
9. **Wade:** confirm he attacks and moves fastest, but each hit feels lighter.
10. **Bark:** confirm startup is slower and readable, while hits, guard pressure, and armor feel powerful.
11. **The Sage/Plouke:** confirm his unusual timing and long reach feel deliberate rather than broken.
12. Report any animation that fails to match when the hitbox becomes active.

## Launch and pursuit

13. Land a Heavy or Launcher and look for **PURSUIT WINDOW**.
14. Press Dash to chase.
15. Press Light for the fast follow-up.
16. Repeat and press Heavy for the slam/wall-bounce route.
17. Confirm only one pursuit happens per combo.
18. Confirm the defender eventually escapes after six airborne hits.
19. Check that damage scaling makes a long combo useful without deleting an entire life unfairly.
20. Try the same route with Wade and Bark; pursuit speed and impact should feel different.

## Defense and Grab

21. Miss Universal Counter on purpose. Its recovery should be clearly punishable.
22. Land Universal Counter and confirm it costs 18 Energy and enters cooldown.
23. Use Combo Breaker while stunned. It should cost 60 Energy and should not repeat immediately.
24. Block repeatedly and verify Perfect Parry remains rewarding.
25. Use Grab against Block at close range.
26. Attempt Grab from too far away and verify the miss is readable and punishable.
27. Confirm Grab does not launch into a free long combo.
28. Confirm Grab never causes or advances a ring-out.

## Energy and specials

29. Begin a new life and confirm Energy starts at 45.
30. Check that the opening does not immediately become Solar Weave or Shots spam.
31. Use Fire Blast, Shots of Agony, Object Swap, Lens, and Solar Weave.
32. Confirm each major technique has readable startup and recovery.
33. Interrupt an opponent during startup. Energy/cooldown should still be committed and the interruption should be obvious.
34. Use Object Swap and punish its arrival recovery.
35. Retreat to extreme range and charge repeatedly. This should be less rewarding than fighting, but not completely useless.

## Shot / Power / Trick readability

36. Check the category labels on Rrvvfo’s hotbar.
37. Confirm Shot, Power, and Trick make sense without reading a long explanation.
38. Open the Sage’s Manual and verify its counterplay advice matches gameplay.
39. Report any technique whose category feels misleading.

## Momentum Finisher

40. Build Momentum through offense, pursuit, taking pressure, and Perfect Parries.
41. Confirm both players can see when Momentum becomes full.
42. Activate full-Momentum Solar Weave.
43. Confirm the warning, startup, projectile, and impact are dramatic.
44. Confirm the move is stronger but does not automatically remove a full life.
45. Try interrupting, avoiding, blocking, or parrying it and report whether the counterplay feels clear.

## Tournament ring-outs

46. Walk near the edge without being hit. Merely standing there must not cause a ring-out.
47. Get hit once or twice near the edge. The HUD should show edge-pressure progress, but no fall should occur yet.
48. Let the attacker complete the sequence with a strong third hit.
49. Confirm the fighter visibly leaves the platform and falls before the KO is awarded.
50. Try using only Grab near the edge; it must never ring out.
51. Respawn and confirm protection prevents an immediate edge sequence.
52. Report any ring-out that happens while the fighter still looks safely on the platform.

## Match pacing and quit risks

53. Play one Quick Battle and one full first-to-three Arena match.
54. Record whether either match becomes repetitive, too defensive, or dominated by running away.
55. Record any move that feels obviously safer and stronger than every alternative.
56. Record any moment where you cannot tell why an attack hit, missed, was blocked, or was interrupted.
57. Record camera discomfort, depth confusion, controller problems, touch problems, slowdown, or audio overlap.
58. Record the fighter and exact move whenever a combo, pursuit, wall bounce, fall, or ability becomes stuck.
