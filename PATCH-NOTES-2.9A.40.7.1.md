# Prototype 2.9A.40.7.1 — Chapter 3 Sabotage Investigation Rewrite

This cumulative patch replaces Chapter 3's old Sage/Plouke-suspicion spine with a sabotage investigation that naturally becomes the first major Project Hollow conspiracy reveal.

## New Chapter 3 spine
- Rrvvfo returns to the damaged tournament because the ring sabotage is unresolved.
- Three arena clues establish deliberate tampering before Sage becomes relevant.
- Tournament worker, security, and the medical worker provide incomplete witness accounts.
- Strange Man warns that the people Rrvvfo is talking to “aren’t the real people.”
- The medical worker's second conversation directly contradicts their first Plouke/maintenance statement.
- Strange Man disappears and leaves his hat; Lens of Truth remains optional and contradictory.
- Tournament maintenance begins ordinary, then gradually exposes unauthorized cables, surveillance, robots, fighter data, and finally the Project Hollow name.
- Sage's trail proves he independently followed the same sabotage mystery.
- The real Sage is found actively overpowering Project Hollow units; danger comes from lockdown, suppression systems, numbers, and Rrvvfo needing to escape rather than Project Hollow simply being stronger than Sage.
- The real Sage stays outside the teleporter room and creates a blue clone to accompany Rrvvfo.
- The teleporter-room lesson is now about the **blue clone principle**, not Object Swap.
- `blueCloneTechniqueFoundationLearned` is mandatory Story progression but unlocks no combat move.
- The unstable teleport sends Rrvvfo to Echo Region, immediately knocks him out, and several days pass while Project Hollow begins its Echo operation.
- Chapter 4 still begins when Rrvvfo wakes and remains uncertain about the real Sage's fate.

## Continuity safeguards
- No early Sage accusation based on the Plouke disguise.
- No Shots of Agony unlock in Chapter 3.
- No reveal of Project Hollow's cloning headquarters, Rrvvfo clone, Revvfo target plan, or full objective.
- Strange Man's identity and allegiance remain unresolved.
- Old Chapter 3 saves migrate into the sabotage rewrite without producing a hybrid old/new ending.
