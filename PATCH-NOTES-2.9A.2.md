# Parallels X Prototype 2.9A.2 — Chapter 2 HUD & Story Menu Hotfix

## Chapter 2 battle HUD

- The large Chapter 2 objective panel no longer covers fighter health, energy, guard, or KO scores.
- During battle, the objective becomes a compact card below the combat HUD.
- The build badge is hidden during Chapter 2 fights.
- Arena Restart and Exit buttons are removed from the live Chapter 2 battle strip; those actions now live in the Story Menu.

## Story Menu

- Training Level and Story XP were moved out of the live fight HUD.
- Added a Chapter 2 Story Menu with:
  - Training Level
  - Story XP and progress bar
  - Combat Manual
  - Restart Active Fight
  - Exit Chapter
  - Return to Game
- Open/close the menu with the onscreen button, Tab, Escape, or M.
- Opening the Story Menu pauses the active arena battle.

## Compatibility

- This is a complete cumulative build containing the 2.9A combat overhaul and 2.9A.1 Story Mode repairs.
