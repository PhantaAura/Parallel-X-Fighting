# Friend Playtest Checklist — Prototype 2.9A.29.2

## Desktop combat

- Trigger Perfect Parry and confirm the green shield cue and guard-bar reaction.
- Break guard and confirm the red shatter cue appears once.
- Use Pursuit Tech and confirm Energy visibly spends without hiding the fight.
- Trigger Wall Splat and Ground Bounce and confirm their icons do not linger.
- Reach full Energy and critical health; confirm each cue occurs once per threshold crossing.
- Let an ability cooldown finish and confirm only that slot pulses.

## Mobile

- Confirm pursuit prompts do not cover health, Energy, the ability row, or touch controls.
- Confirm secondary HUD fades during pursuit and restores afterward.
- Test landscape with the notch/Home indicator on either side.
- Rotate to portrait and confirm the game recommends landscape instead of compressing combat.

## Cleanup

- Pause during an event cue.
- Reset Training during a pursuit.
- End a round during a cue.
- Return to Story or the Main Menu and confirm no icon, bar pulse, or prompt remains.
