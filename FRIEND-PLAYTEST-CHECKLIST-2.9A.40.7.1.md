# Friend Playtest — Chapter 3 Sabotage Investigation Rewrite

Test this once on a fresh normal Story run and once through Chapter 3 Replay.

## Opening mystery
- Does Rrvvfo's reason for returning make immediate sense?
- Can you understand the three damaged-arena clues without feeling like the game is calling Rrvvfo a professional detective?
- After the third clue, is it obvious the sabotage was deliberate?

## Witnesses / Strange Man
- Talk to the tournament worker, Security and the medical worker.
- Confirm the medical worker says Plouke headed toward maintenance.
- Trigger Strange Man and return to the medical worker.
- Confirm the second conversation directly contradicts the first.
- Collect Strange Man's Hat, leave/reload if possible, and verify the hat/progression survives.
- Optional: inspect the hat with Lens of Truth and confirm it creates uncertainty rather than solving the mystery.

## Maintenance / Project Hollow
- Does ordinary tournament maintenance gradually become suspicious instead of instantly looking like a villain base?
- Is Sage's trail readable as “he found this too,” not “he built/sabotaged this”?
- Does the PROJECT HOLLOW name reveal land after the fighter-data discovery?

## Sage / lockdown
- Does the real Sage visibly feel stronger than ordinary Hollow units?
- Does the danger read as lockdown, suppression, numbers and Rrvvfo needing to escape rather than Sage losing a fair fight?

## Blue-clone finale
- Confirm the real Sage stays outside.
- Confirm the blue Sage is visibly separate and gets through with Rrvvfo.
- Confirm Rrvvfo realizes it is a clone.
- Confirm the lesson is about the blue-clone principle.
- Confirm no clone move or Shots of Agony appears in the build/loadout afterward.
- Try an ability during the closing-door beat; it should not turn into an Object Swap lesson.

## Echo handoff
- Confirm the teleport connection disappears.
- Rrvvfo should not get normal free exploration after arrival.
- Several days should pass before Chapter 3 completes.
- Start Chapter 4 and confirm its wake-up/Sage concern still makes sense.
