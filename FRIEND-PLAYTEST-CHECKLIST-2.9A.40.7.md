# Friend Playtest Checklist — Prototype 2.9A.40.7

The goal is not just "can I finish the quest?" Ask whether the player feels like they are solving/routing an objective rather than performing errands.

## Chapter 1
- Do the Main / Forest / Cliff routes feel meaningfully different?
- Does any retained objective still feel like pure padding?

## Chapter 2
- Lost Bracket: can the player naturally discover that two records + Tournament Administration is valid?
- Lost Bracket: does finding all three records remain a legitimate alternate solution?
- Lost Fan: can the player resolve it through Wade OR the fan's family without confusion?
- Does either alternate solution feel obviously worse or pointless?

## Chapter 3
- Do Final Announcement, Cleanup Echoes, Fake Ploukes, and Late Fan feel like short deductions rather than marker cleanup?
- Are wrong answers understandable without becoming annoying?
- Does the investigation rhythm still feel different from Chapter 2's combat-heavy rhythm?

## Chapter 4
- Old Man Potions: try the normal four-catalyst route.
- Old Man Potions: on another run, discover the Apothecary Formula and complete it with two catalysts.
- Does the skilled route feel like knowledge/cleverness rather than an accidental exploit?
- Mountain Signals: choose any two paths and confirm the Hollow Watcher becomes available.
- Confirm the third bearing stays optional.
- Confirm Ryuzankaro remains optional and the floating-lookout ending is unchanged.

## Overall
After each quest, ask:
- Did I move in an interesting way?
- Did I make a decision?
- Did I solve/read something?
- Did combat or character interaction change the rhythm?
- Would I willingly do this objective again on replay?

Any quest that still gets a strong "no" to all five should be a candidate for the 40.8 Adventure Gold Pass.
