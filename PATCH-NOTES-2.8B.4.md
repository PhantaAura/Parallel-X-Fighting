# Prototype 2.8B.4 — Sonic Battle 3-KO Combat

- All normal battles now use a **first-to-3 KOs** format.
- A character respawns after the first and second knockout; the battle ends only after the third.
- CPU opponents now have **135 health per life** in standard CPU and Story combat.
- Chapter 2 opponents now have boosted health based on their original difficulty, with a minimum of 120 health per life.
- Chapter 1’s roadside fight and Chapter 2’s practice fight, optional Bark spar, grunt encounters, qualifier, quarterfinal, and Wade semifinal all require three KOs.
- The Plouke final remains a scripted story-loss encounter so its Fire Awakening attempt, beam clash, and Sage reveal still work.
- Removed timeout victories from the new KO format.
- HUD scores now show progress toward three KOs, and health bars correctly scale above 100 HP.
- Knockouts automatically restart the next life without opening a separate round menu.
