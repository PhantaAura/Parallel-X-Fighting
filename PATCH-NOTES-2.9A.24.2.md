# Prototype 2.9A.24.2 — Hub Camera Comfort

## Changed

- Mouse and Mac trackpad camera look now requires holding the primary click and dragging on the game canvas.
- Moving the pointer by itself no longer rotates the exploration camera.
- Clicking Story menus, dialogue, maps, journals, buttons, and overlays cannot begin a camera drag.
- Exploration camera angles stay where the player leaves them instead of automatically recentering after a short delay.
- Fight framing still takes control and recenters the camera when combat starts.
- Controller right-stick look remains supported in hubs.
- Added **Settings → Gameplay → Hub Free Camera** with **Click and Drag** and **Off** options.
- Added **Camera Look Sensitivity** from 40% to 160%.
- Updated the controls guide, combat manual, help text, and README.

## Compatibility

- Save schema remains 266.
- Chapters 1–4, Strange Man investigation, combat, quests, progression, and fight arenas are unchanged.
