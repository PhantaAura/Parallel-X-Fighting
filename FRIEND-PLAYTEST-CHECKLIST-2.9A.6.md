# Friend Playtest Checklist — Prototype 2.9A.6

The goal is to find confusing, broken, or unpolished behavior—not to be nice to the game.

## Start and menus

- Start once using keyboard, mouse/touch, and controller on separate reloads.
- Confirm the menu opens once and music does not double-start.
- Open Story from the main menu.
- Confirm character select has no second Story Mode option.
- Hover/select every main-menu mode and test the Arcade 3.x message.

## Unified controls

Use the same keyboard or controller in all four places:

1. VS CPU
2. Training
3. Arena
4. Story Chapters 1–3

Check movement, jump, light, heavy, launcher, dash, block, grab/special, charge/breaker, and abilities. Report any screen where the same physical control unexpectedly changes.

## Controller prompts

- Start the Chapter 1 refresher with keyboard.
- Press a controller button and verify the prompt changes.
- Test Nintendo, Xbox, or PlayStation style in Settings.
- Verify Jump, Light, Heavy, Block, Dash, and Charge labels match the selected controller.
- Test the Tournament Road and Chapter 2 QTE Jump button.

## Loading

- Start several different fighter/stage combinations.
- Confirm progress advances through real named tasks.
- Confirm the finished card is readable.
- Interrupt or force a failed load if possible and test Retry once.
- Confirm Retry does not create two games or duplicate audio.
- Confirm Return goes back to the correct menu.

## Story engine

- Complete Chapter 1.
- Continue through the Tournament Road.
- Enter and finish Chapter 2 encounters.
- Open the Chapter 2 Story Menu during and outside fights.
- Enter the Chapter 3 preview.
- Exit and re-enter every chapter.
- Watch for controls, camera, HUD, dialogue, pause, or cleanup behaving differently between chapters.

## Sprites

Test Rrvvfo, Revvfo, and The Sage with Showcase Character Sprites On.

- Check idle, movement, attacks, hitstun, KO, and victory.
- Check that one broken/missing sprite does not disable the other fighters.
- Turn sprites Off and confirm legacy fallback still works.

## Audio

Move quickly between:

- Main menu
- Story/dojo
- Tournament
- Normal battle
- Victory/results

Listen for two songs playing at once, stuck notes, sudden volume jumps, or music that continues after leaving a mode.

## Screen sizes

Check:

- 1440×900 desktop
- 1280×720 laptop
- Landscape phone

Look for overlapping HUD, clipped text, unreachable buttons, accidental scrolling, or artwork blocking taps.

## Report format

For every issue, send:

- Screen or chapter
- Device and browser
- Control method
- What you did
- What happened
- What you expected
- Screenshot or short video when possible
