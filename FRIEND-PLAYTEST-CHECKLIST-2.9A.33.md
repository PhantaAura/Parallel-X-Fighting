# Friend Playtest Checklist — Prototype 2.9A.33

## Quick Battle
1. Complete or unlock Chapter 1 so VS CPU is available.
2. Return to the main menu and choose Quick Battle.
3. Confirm the match starts without reopening the full setup flow.
4. Confirm the match ends after one KO.
5. Return to the menu, use Open Setup, change fighters/stage, and confirm Quick Battle remembers safe selections.

## Hollow Watcher
1. Reach or launch the Chapter 4 Hollow Watcher fight.
2. Repeat one attack until the Watcher labels the pattern as adapted.
3. Confirm repeated adapted hits do reduced—but not zero—damage.
4. Change the move, distance, approach, or attack timing.
5. Confirm `PATTERN BROKEN • WATCHER EXPOSED` appears and the Watcher visibly changes.
6. Attack during the exposed window and confirm the punish feels stronger.
7. Confirm the scan changes at roughly two-thirds and one-third health.

## Stage personality
1. Fight at Resonance Facility and test the center/flank routes around both relay banks.
2. Confirm fighters and projectiles cannot pass through the banks.
3. Fight in Echo Caverns and test curved approaches around all three pillars.
4. Fight on Mountain Path and confirm the outcrops bend routes without making the stage feel boxed in.
5. Fight at the Global Tournament and confirm the official ring remains completely clear.
6. Play a Story fight and confirm the new Arena obstacles do not appear or interfere with chapter scripting.

## Regression
1. Test keyboard/mouse and one controller.
2. Confirm Instant Rematch and Random Rematch still work outside Story.
3. Confirm Random Rematch does not appear in Story.
4. Run `tests/smoke.html` and confirm 322/322 tests pass.
